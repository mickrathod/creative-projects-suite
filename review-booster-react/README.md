## 🛡️ Vision & Product Overview

**ReviewBooster SaaS** is an automated customer reputation platform engineered for physical storefronts, restaurants, dental clinics, salons, auto shops, and hospitality venues. 

For local businesses, a single 1-star Google review can drop search rankings and cost tens of thousands of dollars in lost customer revenue. Most businesses either fail to ask happy customers for reviews or fear that asking customers directly will trigger public complaints.

ReviewBooster solves this through a proprietary **Sentiment-Routing Funnel**:
1. **Happy Customers (4–5 Stars):** Automatically routed straight to the business’s public Google Maps / Yelp profile to leave a glowing 5-star review.
2. **Unhappy Customers (1–3 Stars):** Intelligently intercepted before going public and directed to a private, confidential internal feedback form. The owner receives the feedback immediately in their CRM inbox, allowing them to resolve the issue privately and prevent a devastating 1-star public review.

## 🚀 Key Modules & System Capabilities

### 1. High-Resolution QR Counter Stand Designer
- **Vector-Crisp QR Code Generation:** Live rendering of scannable QR codes encoding the business’s dedicated review funnel URL.
- **Printable Acrylic Table Tent Mode (Ctrl + P):** 1-click transformation into a print-ready, high-resolution physical table tent layout with fold lines, custom logos, call-to-action typography, and NFC tap hints.
- **Dynamic Aesthetic Presets:**
  - *Midnight Luxury:* Deep obsidian black with gold foil accents for fine dining, medspas, and high-end salons.
  - *Emerald Trust:* Clean medical-grade white and mint accents for dental and health clinics.
  - *Neon Electric:* High-energy vibrant styling for modern cafes, barbershops, and fitness studios.

### 2. Smart Sentiment-Routing Funnel (Mobile Simulator)
- **Zero-App Customer Experience:** Works natively on any iOS or Android smartphone camera without requiring app downloads.
- **Interactive 1–5 Star Rating Screen:** Large, responsive star rating cards with haptic animation.
- **Intelligent Branching Logic:**
  - *Rating $\ge$ 4 Stars:* Triggers celebratory confetti animation and redirects to Google Maps with pre-copied praise.
  - *Rating $\le$ 3 Stars:* Shifts into empathy mode: *"We're so sorry your experience wasn't exceptional. How can our management make this right?"*

### 3. Private Feedback Intercept CRM Inbox
- **Confidential Complaint Management:** Displays all intercepted 1–3 star submissions with customer name, phone number, timestamp, and detailed complaint text.
- **Resolution Workflow:** Business owners can mark feedback as *Investigating*, *In Progress*, or *Resolved*, complete with internal manager notes.
- **Sentiment Analytics & KPI Cards:** Live overview of total scans, positive review percentage (%), intercepted complaints, and estimated protected revenue.

### 4. Agency Sales Pitch & Pricing Blueprints
- **Turnkey Client Acquisition Materials:** Built-in objection-handling scripts, ROI calculators, and agency sales presentations demonstrating how to package this service to local merchants for $199 setup plus $49/month recurring retainer.
